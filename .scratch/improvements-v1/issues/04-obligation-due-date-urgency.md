# 04 — Obligation Due Date Urgency and Fast Timeline Filter Reset

**What to build:** Enhance obligations and installment cards with visual countdown badges showing days remaining (e.g. "۲ روز مانده", "امروز سررسید است", "سررسید گذشته"). In the timeline, show an active filter bar with transaction match count and a one-tap clear button when search or filters are active.
